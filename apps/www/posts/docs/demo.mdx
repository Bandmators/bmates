The interactive editor now lives on the [BMates home page](/#live-demo), alongside the introduction to the library.

You can play the project, mute or remove tracks, move clips on the timeline, and inspect the exported project data.

> [!NOTE]
> Audio playback requires a browser interaction. The first press of **Play** resumes the browser audio context.
